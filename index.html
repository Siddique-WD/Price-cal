Important assumptions

  • This is an estimate, not an official Etsy payout statement.
  • Listing fees are entered manually in your selected currency. Etsy's $0.20 listing fee is converted using the applicable rate.
  • Offsite Ads are charged only when a sale qualifies. Enter the applicable fee cap in the selected currency.
  • Buyer taxes collected and remitted by Etsy are not automatically treated as your income or as a cost here.
  • GST on seller fees, GST on your own sales, input tax credits, income tax, refunds and cross-border tax obligations may need separate accounting advice.
  • Shipping charged to the buyer is included in the order-based percentage fees. Your actual shipping cost is a separate expense.
